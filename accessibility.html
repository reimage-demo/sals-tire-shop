Accessibility statement

Last updated October 13, 2026

Access to shop information

We aim to make Sal’s Tire Shop’s hours, address, services, prices, and telephone number easy to find and use. Our design target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This is a design goal, not a claim of independent certification or complete conformance.

Features on this site

  • Semantic headings, clearly labeled links, and a skip-to-content link.
  • Image descriptions included in image markup for assistive technology, without visible captions underneath photographs.
  • Keyboard focus indicators and controls that use native browser interactions.
  • Responsive layouts and text that can be enlarged with browser settings.
  • Reduced-motion support and essential shop information available without JavaScript.

Photo viewer

Select a gallery photograph to open it. Use the Close photo button or Escape to dismiss the viewer. Keyboard focus returns to the photograph you selected. Without JavaScript, gallery links open the original image.

Help and feedback

If you encounter a barrier, call the shop or write to the address below. If possible, tell us which page or task was difficult and the browser or assistive technology you used. Do not include passwords or sensitive information. You can also request shop information or discuss service directly by phone.

Third-party services

Google Maps and other linked websites are controlled by their providers and may work differently. Call Sal’s if you need help with directions or information shown on this site.
