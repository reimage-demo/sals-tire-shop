Scope

This notice describes the public Sal’s Tire Shop website hosted on GitHub Pages. It covers browsing this site and using its contact links.

Information on this website

This site has no account registration, contact form, online checkout, advertising pixels, or site-installed analytics. Its code does not set cookies or use local storage. Fonts and images are served with the website rather than loaded from advertising services.

Hosting information

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may process technical information such as an IP address and request information to deliver and secure its services. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices, retention, and privacy options. Sal’s does not control GitHub’s infrastructure logs.

Calling the shop

The call button opens your phone service; this website does not record or transcribe calls. If you choose to call Sal’s, you may share your name, callback number, tire size, or service question directly with the shop. Share only what is needed for your inquiry. Questions about information provided by phone or in person should be directed to Sal’s.

External links

Directions open Google Maps. The website credit opens REIMAGE BUSINESS SOLUTIONS. Those services apply their own privacy practices when you visit them; a map is not embedded on this website.

Privacy questions and requests

Contact Sal’s to ask about information you provided directly to the shop or to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Contact GitHub for requests concerning information it processes independently. This notice does not restrict rights available under applicable law.
